# glassAI
# What is glassAI?
GlassAI is a software suite that automates many routine tasks, such as sales, inventory management, or installation scheduling, using artificial intelligence.
# What does it solve?
glassAI streamlines processes such as sales, predicting stock needs, or providing production updates by making it fully automatic, using artificial intelligence systems to assist with minimal or no need to supervise the system.
# Why is it unique?
glassAI is unique to the glass industry because it brings many commercial-grade tools to get customers, manage inventory, predict stock needs, provide production updates, and auto-schedule installations, to small and medium glass shops.
glassAI is one of the first all-in-one software suites to use AI in this way specifically tailored for glass shops.

# How it works
saleAI is one of our tools, which uses a customer's previous quotes with AI to determine a quote price without any human contact or delay. saleAI detects urgency, hesitation on an order or what the customer or contractor is attracted to and gives recommendations and generates time-limited discounts for products that the customer or contractor may be interested in. Prices are also auto-adjusted by both market demand, customer demand, current inventory, and rush delivery status using artificial intelligence.
cueAI predicts inventory and production needs using past sale counts and AI systems to manage the company’s inventory easily and reduce costs such as inventory taxes* or loss of customers due to lack of inventory.
cueAI can also predict maintenance needs and use saleAI to auto-adjust pricing. 
cueAI also integrates AI systems to auto-order stock, make prices higher using saleAI or as a last resort switch suppliers in times in which supplier delays or geopolitical issues are expected.
oStat combines the power of AI and data pulled from other programs, like A+W or Optima, to determine live production status, which is handed off to the customer through a web platform. It can predict delays using maintenance schedules, inventory, and other factors. oStat can also use quality control systems and give a score to the customer of how good their glass is expected to be.
insAI uses AI to predict installation times based on job size, location, and complexity, including factoring in weather and local contractor availability. This integrates with the customer's web portal or mobile app to display updates on the ETA for installation. 
kiAI is an AI-powered system designed for sale kiosks, being an extension of saleAI. The customer can describe for what is the glass going to be used and AI then determines the customer’s glass needs and the configuration of the glass. A key feature is the ability to create a quote for any project at home using the web or mobile apps, then simply scanning a QR code in the kiosk to be able to sign-in automatically and finalize the order process in person.
